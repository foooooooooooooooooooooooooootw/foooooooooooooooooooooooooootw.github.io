The Darkroom

04 / The DarkroomWhere the image is made

Welcome to the archive. I'm a photographer chasing the same two subjects the old future always promised us: the ever-shifting sky, and the city — its concrete, its glass, its quiet corners.

Every frame is a transmission. Photography is how I hold onto moments that slip past in the noise of daily life; once exposed, a moment is fixed forever — you can never develop the same instant twice.

Mostly skies and old buildings. I'm drawn to structures built for a future we never quite got — the kind being torn down to make room for glass towers that promise the same thing.

I aim for the uncanny, the almost-fantasy kind of stillness — empty streets, no people — so a place feels like it's waiting for someone who isn't there yet.

It's also a correction. I was born with Antimetropia and astigmatism in both eyes, so a lens is the only way I've ever been able to see the world the way everyone else does — one frame at a time.

If any of it makes you look up, look closer, notice the ordinary turning into something worth keeping — then the archive did its job. The real exhibition is happening outside, every single day.
