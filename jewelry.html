Where the pieces came from

Two of these began as old jewelry I found without a stone. I carried them home, chose a stone to match, and cut it to fit. Every stone here was shaped by hand, so the shine sometimes only shows from one angle.

Provenance

LAPIS Band from Chatuchak Market, Bangkok. Stone possibly Afghan.

MOON Brass band from a night market in Malaysia.

CHAROITE Pendant found in Thailand without its stone. Charoite is only mined in Siberia.
