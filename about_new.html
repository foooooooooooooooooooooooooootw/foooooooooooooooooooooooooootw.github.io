02 / The Operator

When I'm not stargazing, I enjoy working with my hands. The camera is usually in my pocket, the soldering iron is in the bench drawer, and the terminal is never closed.

This site is my small sky — five rooms, each one a different way of making things. Film, metal, stone, code, and the quiet places in between. I build slowly, by hand, and I try to keep the shine on the work rather than the packaging.

Each room opens its own page, and hopefully more in the future. If you find something here that resonates, or if you want to say hello, the coordinates are at the bottom of this manifest.
